Format & Scoring Rules

Auto-Enforced

Covers the standard ATHE assignment format and scoring. Word exports follow your format profile in Settings, which is prefilled with exactly these values.

  • Font Type & Size: Times New Roman (14pt headings / 12pt body)
  • Line & Para Spacing: 1.5 line, before 0pt, after 0pt
  • Page Margins: Left 1.5in, Top / Right / Bottom 1.0in
  • Headings & Page No: Left aligned headings, page numbers bottom center
  • Required Word Count: 2,000 to 3,500 words
  • Turnitin Thresholds: Similarity at or under 20%, AI report required (from the tab 4 email pre-check)
  • APA Referencing Rules:
    • Text Books: Author (Year), Name of textbook, Edition, Publisher.
    • Journal: Author (Year), Title of paper, Name of Journal, Vol ( ) No ( ), Page No.
    • Website: http... (Retrieved date).

Voice Card, Rules Applied

  1. Zero AI Cliché Vocabulary. Automatically strips words commonly flagged as AI clichés: delve, tapestry, underscore, testament, pivotal, holistic, paradigm, spearhead, utilize.
  2. Natural Sentence Rhythm. Mixes concise declarative sentences with detailed analytical explanations and practical business examples (for example the cases and figures in your brief).
  3. Grounded Lecture Integration. Keeps the brief's own formulas and worked figures in view (payback, contribution, the three-column cash book, the imprest petty cash system) instead of generic filler.
